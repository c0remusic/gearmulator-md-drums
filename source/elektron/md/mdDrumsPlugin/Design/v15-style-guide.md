# MD Drums style guide, v15

The guides of v8 to v14 hold; v15 sets the sizes on the golden ratio.

## Rules

- **φ in the details, symmetry in the structure** (Figma's guide on the golden ratio): the three pages stay equal; their
  proportions, the screens' and the type follow φ, each size the nearest one on the 40 x 20 grid.
- **Golden rectangles**: a page is 320 x 520, a screen 288 x 180.

## Type (Fibonacci: 13, 21, 34)

| Level | Use | Size, weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Machine name in the head | 34 px regular, ink |
| 2 | Page titles, browser title, preview name | 21 px semibold |
| 3 | Values, facts in the head, what a family is | 13 px |
| 4 | Labels, group and family names, captions | 11 px (the floor: 13/φ^0.5 = 10.2, rounded up to stay legible) |
| 5 | Hints, footer, graduation labels | 11 px, ink-faint |
