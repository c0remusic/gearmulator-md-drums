# MD Drums style guide, v18

The guides of v8 to v17 hold; v18 adds one rule.

## Rules

- **The categories are framed**: one rule between two bands, across the editing width, over the first and under the
  last. The screens fill their band's cell, so those rules are their frame, with one rule on their left at x 880.
  These are the only full-width rules; inside a band, only the group rules over the knobs.
