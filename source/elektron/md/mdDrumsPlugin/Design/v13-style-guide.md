# MD Drums style guide, v13

The guides of v8 to v12 hold; v13 adds one rule.

## Rules

- **A view earns its tab**: it offers a choice, or shows what no other view shows. A fact that belongs to a control
  sits on that control (the output under MIX's channel), and how to use it sits in its tooltip.
- Name things as the host names them where the user meets them there: "Out 01", "Main".
