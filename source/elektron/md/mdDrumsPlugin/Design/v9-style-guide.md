# MD Drums style guide, v9

v8's guide holds (`v8-style-guide.md`); v9 adds one rule and changes the head.

## Rules

- **Dark names and picks, light edits.** Chrome (#1b1c1e) carries the top bar, the track list and the selected track's
  head band; the surface (#26272a) carries only the pages that edit the sound; screens are darker still (#151618).
  The edge between chrome and surface is the demarcation: no rule or box is added to it.
- **The head on the pages' columns**: what the track is over SYN, what it is made of and where it goes over EFX, what
  you do with it over ROUTING's slots.
- A line under the top bar separates the plug-in's bar from the track's band, both chrome.
