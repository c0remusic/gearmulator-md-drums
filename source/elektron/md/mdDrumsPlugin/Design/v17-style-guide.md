# MD Drums style guide, v17

The guides of v8 to v16 hold; v17 changes the pages' arrangement.

## Rules

- **A page is a band**: title, a rule over its knobs (group names where its groups are fixed), its eight knobs in the
  Machinedrum's order, its screen right after them. The three bands share every vertical edge.
- **A screen belongs to its band**: same top and bottom, 32 px after the last knob, the space between two knobs.
- **φ between doing and seeing**: knobs 640 px, screens 400 px.
