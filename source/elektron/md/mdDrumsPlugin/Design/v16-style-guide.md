# MD Drums style guide, v16

The guides of v8 to v15 hold; v16 changes the window and the space around the pages.

## Spacing

- Window 1280 x 800 (1.600).
- 60 px of space above the pages and 60 px under them; 40 px between the pages' columns stays.
- A golden size is never stretched to fill a larger window: the space grows around it.
