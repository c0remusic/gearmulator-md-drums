# MD Drums v22 — style guide

v21's (type 12 / 14 / 22 / 34, case, knobs, controls, words), with:

- **Colour roles:** ink = content, and a chosen option on the panel; ink-dim = secondary text; ink-faint = values at
  their default, hints, axis labels; `--track` = every control's border, and a waveform not yet heard; accent = what
  is live (the shown track, the open tab, the playhead, a modulation, ASSIGN armed, meters) and, on the screens, what
  the controls draw there: the curves they set and the options chosen on a screen.
- **Screens:** a plot's axis says what it measures: time in ms under the hit, frequencies and 0 dB on the filter, the
  target's 0 and 127 and its knob's value on the LFO. Labels sit on their line, or in the rows' label column.
- **Controls on a screen's title:** words, 14 px ink, the panel's fill on hover; no frame. A control says what a click
  does now (Assign, then Cancel).
- **Play key:** lights while pressed only; never on a hit.
- **Toggles:** M, S and Out share one look: `--track` border, 12 px semibold; on, Mute in its colour, Solo and Out in
  ink.
