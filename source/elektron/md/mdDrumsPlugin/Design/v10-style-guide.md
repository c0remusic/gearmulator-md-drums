# MD Drums style guide, v10

v8's and v9's guides hold (`v8-style-guide.md`, `v9-style-guide.md`); v10 adds two rules and changes the head.

## Rules

- **Say a name once per level.** A name appears in the list that picks it and in the title that shows it, not again
  in a bar, a subtitle or a breadcrumb.
- **A control sits next to what it acts on**, then on the grid: alignment chooses among the places that make sense,
  never the place itself.
- One title per view: one size step above everything else, used once.
